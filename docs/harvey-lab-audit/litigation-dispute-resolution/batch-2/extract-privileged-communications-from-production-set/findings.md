Batch C contains an additional express request to counsel: July 22 email, Bates RDGL-00020346, ¶216, asks Ray and Tom whether the study can be used. July 26 reply, Bates RDGL-00020343, ¶125–127, conveys the answer and implementation of counsel's advice. These passages require separate analysis/redaction; they are not purely sales business merely because they lie outside the central Ochoa/Viklund pair (RDGL-00020344–345). Repair: require identification of the central pair, permit additional advice-seeking or advice-relaying passages to be protected, and produce severable business content. The core confidentiality/legal-purpose rule is described in the Third Circuit's Teleglobe decision: https://www2.ca3.uscourts.gov/opinarch/062915p.pdf, discussion of attorney-client privilege and communications within corporations. This does not imply every employee reaction to advice is privileged; the blanket nonprivileged requirement is the defect.
- **C-019/020/022 — unsupported fixed work-product split and false chronology (moderate/high).** C-020 says slides 1–8 predate any litigation threat and must be non-work-product. Batch G metadata ¶8–11 says the whole presentation was created December 5, 2023. Routine subject matter is not the same as a document created in ordinary business independent of litigation: counsel may compile background facts for litigation. Work-product analysis turns on preparation in anticipation of litigation, not whether every paragraph explicitly discusses litigation. In re Cendant, https://www2.ca3.uscourts.gov/opinarch/024386p.pdf, pp7–13. Partial treatment is defensible and supported by the original coding in QC report ¶123; the error is failing a reasoned broader claim or requiring invented pre-threat creation. Attorney-client protection for legal advice in slides 1–8 must also remain available. C-064 correctly requires discussing the CID, but should not demand that it was the first possible anticipation date.
- **C-038 — unsupported earlier separate-retention fact (moderate).** The October 8–9 Correa/Nagarajan exchange (batch I ¶17–27,49–59) supplies personal legal advice and confidentiality. It does not say Correa had already retained Kendrick Sable at that time. Separate representation is documented in the November 2 batch F and later documents; no October retention date is supplied.
