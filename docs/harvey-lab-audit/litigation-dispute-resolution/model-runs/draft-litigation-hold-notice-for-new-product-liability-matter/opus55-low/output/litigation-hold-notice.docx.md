# 1. Why You Are Receiving This Notice

On May 28, 2025, Vantage Medical Devices, Inc. (“Vantage” or the “Company”) was served with a putative class action complaint filed in federal court on behalf of persons in the United States implanted with the ProFlex KR-3000 Total Knee Replacement System (“KR-3000”). The complaint asserts claims relating to the design, testing, regulatory clearance, manufacture, marketing, sale, and post-market performance of the KR-3000. The Company disputes the allegations and intends to defend itself vigorously.

Because of this lawsuit, the Company and its employees are **legally required to preserve** all information that may be relevant to the claims or defenses in the case. You are receiving this notice because Company records indicate that you have, or have access to, information that may be relevant. **Receiving this notice does not mean that you have done anything wrong.** It means that the information you hold is important and must be protected.

This notice is issued under Section 7 of the Company’s Document Retention and Destruction Policy, VNT-POL-007, Rev. 3. **It overrides every normal retention period, auto-delete setting, destruction schedule, clean-up practice, device refresh, system migration, and offboarding procedure** that would otherwise apply to covered information. It remains in effect until you receive written notice from the General Counsel that it has been lifted — even if the lawsuit appears to be over, and even if you change roles or leave the Company.

# 2. Time Period Covered

Preserve covered information created, sent, received, or modified from **January 1, 2017 through the present, and going forward** for as long as this hold is in effect. Information created after today is covered too. If you have older material related to the KR-3000 or its predecessor (the ProFlex KR-2500) that informed KR-3000 design or testing, preserve it as well.

# 3. Subject Matter Covered

Preserve **all** information that refers or relates to any of the following. When in doubt, preserve it and ask.

- **Design and development** of the KR-3000, including design inputs/outputs, design history files, design reviews, CAD (SolidWorks) files, finite element analysis (FEA), materials selection, surface finish and polishing specifications, and engineering change orders.

- **Testing**, including bench, wear, cyclical-loading, and accelerated wear simulation testing (e.g., ASTM F3141 / ISO 14243), biocompatibility, and any comparison to the KR-2500 or competitor devices.

- **Regulatory matters**, including the 510(k) submission K192847 and its preparation, predicate-device comparisons, FDA correspondence and meetings, Medical Device Reports (MDRs), corrections and removals (21 CFR Part 806) evaluations, inspections, and labeling / Instructions for Use.

- **Metallurgical, failure, retrieval, or surface-characterization analyses** of KR-3000 components, including the analysis of retrieved femoral components referenced in the complaint as having occurred in or around July–September 2022, and any related reports, drafts, data, images, and communications.

- **Quality system records**: complaints, complaint trending, revision-rate analyses, CAPAs (opened, considered, or declined), nonconformances, deviations, health hazard evaluations, risk management files, field action or recall evaluations, and post-market surveillance.

- **Manufacturing** at Fort Wayne and at Ashford Precision Components, Inc. (Grand Rapids, Michigan): batch/device history records, process validation (IQ/OQ/PQ), incoming material inspection and certifications (CoCrMo, UHMWPE, titanium alloy), in-process and final inspection, surface finish measurements, supplier audits, and the Quality Agreement (QA-2017-0044).

- **Sales, marketing, and field communications**: promotional claims, brochures, surgeon training, launch materials, sales communications with surgeons, hospitals, and OR staff, field complaint reports, Salesforce records, and VantagePulse records.

- **Clinical and medical affairs**: surgeon advisory board and key opinion leader (KOL) communications, clinical evaluation reports, post-market clinical follow-up, literature reviews, and adverse event clinical assessments.

- **Executive, board, and financial matters**: leadership and board discussions of KR-3000 performance, complaints, recall or field action options, revenue impact, warranty reserves, product liability accruals, and insurance.

- **The named plaintiffs** (Dorothy M. Kessler and Raymond A. Dufresne), their implanting and treating surgeons and hospitals (including Riverside Methodist Hospital, Columbus, Ohio, and the implanting facility in Tampa, Florida), and any device lot, complaint, or returned product associated with them.

- **Training and HR records** related to KR-3000 product or surgeon training, and any ethics hotline or internal report concerning KR-3000 quality or safety.

- Do not delete, shred, or discard documents, even duplicates or drafts, and do not empty “Deleted Items,” recycle bins, or trash folders.

- Do not run disk clean-up, file-shredding, or “optimization” software on any device containing covered information.

- Do not modify, obsolete, retire, or archive Veeva Vault documents, or allow drafts to lapse, relating to the KR-3000 without Legal Department authorization.

- Do not move, rename, reorganize, or annotate existing files, or delete Salesforce records or activity notes.

- Do not send KR-3000 records or paper files to off-site storage (including Iron Mountain) without Legal Department clearance.

- Do not forward covered information to personal accounts, and do not attempt to gather or copy documents yourself unless the Legal Department asks you to.

# 8. Confidentiality and Communications About the Lawsuit

This notice is a confidential communication from the Legal Department. Please do not forward it outside the Company. Do not discuss the lawsuit or this notice with anyone outside the Company (including plaintiffs, their lawyers, surgeons, customers, or the media) and refer any inquiries about the lawsuit to the Legal Department. Please avoid creating new documents, emails, or messages commenting on the lawsuit or speculating about the issues in it; if you have concerns or relevant information, bring them directly to the Legal Department.

**Protected reporting.** Nothing in this notice prohibits or restricts you from reporting possible violations of law to, communicating with, or providing information to any government agency (including the FDA, SEC, or Department of Justice), or from exercising any right protected by law. You do not need the Company’s permission to do so. Normal business operations — including complaint handling, MDR reporting, and regulatory obligations — must continue as usual.

# 9. Privileged Materials

This hold applies to communications with lawyers (in-house or outside counsel), legal memoranda, and other privileged or work-product materials. **Preserve them like everything else.** Preservation does not mean production: privileged materials will be collected and reviewed under separate procedures supervised by the Legal Department and outside counsel and will not be disclosed without appropriate privilege review.

# 10. If You Change Roles or Leave the Company

Your obligations continue if you transfer roles. If you plan to leave the Company, notify the Legal Department **as soon as you give notice**. Before your last day, the Legal Department will arrange preservation of your devices and accounts and a brief exit interview. Do not return, wipe, or dispose of any device, and do not remove, delete, or take any covered information. Human Resources and IT will not process standard offboarding for any person subject to this hold without Legal Department clearance.

# 11. If Information May Already Have Been Lost

If you know or suspect that any covered information has been deleted, lost, wiped, overwritten, or altered — at any time, before or after this notice (for example, through a device replacement, an auto-delete setting, or a routine clean-up) — tell the Legal Department **immediately**. Do not try to recover or recreate it yourself. Prompt reporting allows the Company to take recovery steps; it will be treated as cooperation.

# 12. Duration, Reminders, and Compliance

This hold remains in effect until lifted in writing by the General Counsel. You will receive periodic reminders (the first on or about August 1, 2025, and at least quarterly thereafter) and may be asked to confirm continued compliance or to participate in an interview or data collection. Compliance with this notice is mandatory. Failure to comply can expose the Company to serious court sanctions and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, consistent with VNT-POL-007, Section 8.
