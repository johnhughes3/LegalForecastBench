# Executive summary

Vantage was served May 28, 2025 with a putative class action alleging that the ProFlex KR-3000 knee system was defectively designed/manufactured and that Vantage failed to disclose or respond to elevated wear, complaints, and adverse outcomes. The complaint specifically identifies 2017–2019 testing, a Q3 2022 metallurgical analysis, post-market quality/regulatory records, manufacturing records at Vantage and Ashford, and informal field communications. We should preserve relevant material from January 1, 2017 through the present, on a rolling basis.

The most immediate risk is irreversible loss during scheduled systems and personnel changes: SAP ECC 6.0 migration begins June 16 with legacy archival/decommissioning planned by July 15; Petrosian departs June 20 and is subject to ordinary reimage/account shutdown steps; 30 iPhones are scheduled for wipe beginning June 23; M365 email auto-purge runs June 30. Teams, Veeva draft versions, mobile-local data, and backup tapes also face routine deletion/overwrite. The proposed hold notice is drafted for distribution June 2; Legal and IT must implement technical controls immediately.

| **BOTTOM LINE:** Issue the hold immediately; suspend destructive retention and lifecycle operations; preserve source data and metadata before any migration, offboarding, wipe, or purge; and do not authorize SAP decommissioning or device wiping without written General Counsel sign-off. |

# 6. Preservation, collection, and privilege safeguards

**•** Separate preservation from discovery collection: source preservation begins now. Collect in a forensically sound manner, with chain-of-custody records, hashes, time zone/source details, tool/operator logs, and data validation. Preserve native formats and metadata; do not rely on PDF renditions or routine backups where they omit audit trails or mobile-local content.

**•** For the General Counsel’s own files, apply preservation holds but use an outside-counsel supervised, segregated collection/review environment. Designate a privilege coordinator; screen before substantive review or production; maintain privilege log protocol. Seek a Federal Rule of Evidence 502(d) order/clawback protection at the Rule 26(f) or scheduling conference. Privileged materials must not be disclosed to IT, vendor reviewers, insurers, or opposing counsel beyond need-to-know instructions without Legal review.

**•** Require all recipients to acknowledge receipt, confirm known repositories and mobile/personal use, and report known deletion or system changes. Keep an auditable log of notices, reminders, system holds, migrations, exports, chain of custody, exceptions, and remedial steps. Conduct interviews and verification rather than treating a notice as sufficient by itself.

**•** Do not restore backup tapes at this stage. Preserve tape media and catalogs as a secondary safeguard. Assess accessibility and restore only if source-level collection identifies a gap or discovery supports restoration; document the burden and unique data value.
