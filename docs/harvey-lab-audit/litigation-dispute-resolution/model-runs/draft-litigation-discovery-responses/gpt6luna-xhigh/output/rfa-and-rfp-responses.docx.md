# I. GENERAL RESPONSE AND ESI PROTOCOL

1\. The General Objections below apply where relevant; they are not intended as a substitute for a request-specific response. Prismavale asserts only those specific objections stated in the response to the particular request. Prismavale will answer the unobjectionable portion of a request and will identify the scope of any limitation.

2\. RFA subpart-count objection. The parties’ Joint Discovery Stipulation limits each side to 30 RFAs and permits the receiving party, at its discretion, to count compound RFAs with distinct subparts as multiple requests; a dispute must be addressed through meet and confer and the stipulation does not automatically disqualify a request. Requests 14, 15, and 16 each contain multiple discrete propositions. If their subparts are counted separately, the 25 numbered requests comprise 32 matters. Prismavale preserves this limited objection and requests a prompt meet and confer; nevertheless, it responds to every subpart on the merits to avoid any question of waiver or deemed admission.

3\. ESI form. Under the Joint Discovery Stipulation, Prismavale will produce responsive ESI in native format where practicable. Spreadsheets, ERP exports, databases, and structured data will be produced natively to preserve formulas, structures, and relationships. Email will be produced as .msg/.eml (or equivalent native format) where practicable; if a category is produced as single-page TIFF, the production will include load files with extracted text (OCR text for redactions) and the stipulated email and document metadata, and the response/production letter will identify why native production is not practicable for that category. Other non-native ESI will be produced as single-page TIFF with available metadata and extracted text. Bates numbers will use PRISMAVALE; a Bates-numbered placeholder/cover sheet will accompany native files and the native filename will include its Bates range. De-duplication, if used, will be by MD5 or SHA-1 hash and the source custodians will be identified.

4\. Privilege. Prismavale will withhold privileged or work-product material and provide the stipulated Category A or Category B log, as applicable, within 30 days after each production, subject to the parties’ protocol and any entered Fed. R. Evid. 502(d) order. Inadvertent production will be addressed under the operative clawback order, if entered.

5\. Production timing and organization. Responsive documents will be produced as kept in the ordinary course or organized and labeled by request, under Fed. R. Civ. P. 34(b)(2)(E). Commitments below mean that Prismavale will conduct a reasonable search of sources within its possession, custody, or control and produce nonprivileged responsive material located after that search. No response represents that production has already occurred.
