# PART ONE — DEFENDANT PRISMAVALE CHEMICAL SOLUTIONS, LLC’S RESPONSES AND OBJECTIONS TO PLAINTIFF’S FIRST SET OF REQUESTS FOR ADMISSION (NOS. 1–25)

Defendant Prismavale Chemical Solutions, LLC (“Prismavale”), through its counsel Hollister & Marsh LLP, responds and objects to Plaintiff Greenleaf Organics, Inc.’s (“Greenleaf”) First Set of Requests for Admission (Nos. 1–25). Those Requests were served May 5, 2025. Prismavale responds under Fed. R. Civ. P. 36 and the parties’ Joint Stipulation Regarding Discovery Procedures dated April 28, 2025 (the “Stipulation”).

## PRELIMINARY STATEMENT

1.  These responses are based on Prismavale’s reasonable inquiry and on the information known or readily obtainable by Prismavale as of this date. Prismavale will supplement them as required by Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(e).
2.  An admission is made only for purposes of this action. It is not an admission for any other purpose or proceeding (Fed. R. Civ. P. 36(b)). By responding, Prismavale does not concede relevance or admissibility.
3.  Where Prismavale objects, it states the objection specifically in response to the individual Request. Where Prismavale responds subject to an objection, it states what it admits and what it denies or qualifies, as Rule 36(a)(4) requires.

## OBJECTIONS TO DEFINITIONS AND INSTRUCTIONS

**A. “Contamination Events” (Definition 7).** Prismavale objects to this definition because it assumes disputed facts. Specifically, it assumes that the SB-102 and PS-302 lots were contaminated as shipped by Prismavale. It also misdescribes the Thornfield Analytical Labs findings. As Greenleaf itself pleads (Compl. ¶ 43), the August 15, 2024 Thornfield report stated a *total aerobic microbial count* of 450 CFU/g and identified the *presence* of *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*. It did not report *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* “at 450 CFU/g.” Prismavale’s responses do not adopt this definition. Any use of the term in a response refers only to the test results that Greenleaf reported.

**B. “You,” “Your,” or “Prismavale” (Definition 2).** Prismavale objects to this definition to the extent it includes Prismavale’s attorneys. Prismavale responds on behalf of Prismavale Chemical Solutions, LLC.

**C. Instruction No. 1 (“under oath”).** Prismavale objects to this Instruction to the extent it requires verified answers. Fed. R. Civ. P. 36(a)(3) requires a written answer or objection “signed by the party or its attorney.” It does not require an oath. These responses are signed by counsel under Rules 26(g) and 36.

**D. Numerical limit (Stipulation § 2(a)).** Stipulation § 2(a) limits each side to thirty Requests for Admission and permits the receiving party to count compound requests with distinct subparts as multiple requests. Requests Nos. 14, 15, and 16 contain ten separately enumerated factual propositions, so the First Set amounts to at least thirty-two Requests for Admission. Requests Nos. 6, 8, 9, and 10 are also compound. Prismavale objects that the First Set exceeds the stipulated limit. Without waiving that objection, and to avoid unnecessary motion practice, Prismavale responds to each subpart below. Prismavale’s position is that Greenleaf has exhausted its allotment of Requests for Admission and may not serve more without leave of Court under Stipulation § 2(d).
