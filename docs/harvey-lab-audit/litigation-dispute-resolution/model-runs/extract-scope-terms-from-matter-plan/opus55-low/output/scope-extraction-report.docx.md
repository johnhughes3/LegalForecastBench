# 1. Purpose, Sources and Method

This report pulls out every scope-defining term in the engagement materials: parties and matters, work streams, exclusions, budgets and caps, rates, staffing, approval thresholds, reporting, success fee and termination terms, and governing-document hierarchy. It then compares each term across all six source documents and flags every place where they disagree. Each flag gets a severity rating, a view on which document controls, and a recommended fix.

*Which document controls: EL §§11–12 say the EL controls over both the OCGs and the MP. MP §10 ranks them EL \> OCG \> MP. # 2. Executive Summary

We identified 42 inconsistencies or open items: 12 HIGH, 17 MEDIUM and 13 LOW. The most significant are:

- WS-4 (CPSC) was added without the approval the EL requires. Its \$320,000 sub-budget is above the 5% new-work-stream limit (\$212,500) in EL §§1, 7 and 12. That limit requires the General Counsel's prior written re-approval. Revision 1 was agreed and signed only by the Billing Partner and the Deputy GC. We found no GC approval in the record.

- The work-stream budgets do not agree across the documents. The MP and the XLS both total \$4,250,000, but they split it differently: WS-1 is \$1.85M vs \$1.65M, WS-6 is \$420K vs \$870K, WS-7 is \$345K vs \$450K, and the MP includes a \$360K contingency that the XLS drops. In addition, the XLS Monthly Forecast columns do not add up to the XLS's own stated totals.

- The expert and disbursement budget has a \$20,000 gap. The EL and MP §3.3 set expert fees at \$780,000 (rebuttal reserve \$230,000). MP §7.2 and the XLS use \$760,000 (reserve \$210,000), which brings the disbursement total to \$1,330,000 against the \$1,350,000 the EL approved.

- The documents disagree on whether a fee-cap overage can be approved after the fact. The EL allows retroactive Deputy GC approval of overages up to 10%, within 15 business days of the invoice. The MP shortens that window to 5 business days after month-end. OCG §4.3 bans retroactive approval outright, with no de minimis exception. Because of the hierarchy conflict, it is unclear which rule applies.

- "Aggregate payments", the term that drives the success fee, is still undefined. The emails show the parties disagreed (the firm wanted indemnity-only; the client wanted total cost including carrier payments) and never resolved it. Neither the EL nor the MP defines the term.

- There is still a gap between the non-HX-9000 exclusion (EX-6) and WS-1. EX-6 carves consolidated MDL claims back in, but WS-1 covers only the MDL "as currently constituted". The MP says the carve-back does not authorize any work. The EL's version of EX-6 is broader than the MP's.

- WS-5 conflicts with the EL on carrier contact. The MP authorizes "direct communication" with Fortitude and Ridgeline about coverage positions. EL §8 prohibits that without the Deputy GC's prior coordination and approval.

- WS-4 is described inconsistently. Revision 1 calls the firm "coordinating counsel only", with no direct CPSC contact, yet lists VCAP submission to the CPSC and attendance at CPSC meetings as in scope. VCAP work also sits close to EX-4 (recall advice).

- The contract reviewer cost has no budget line. The EL says it is billed separately and sits outside the blended rates. The MP calls it a disbursement but gives it no line. The XLS puts it inside the WS-6 fee budget. The OCGs require every reviewer to be approved by name.

- The "February 10, 2025" Matter Plan already contains Revision 1 content (the March 28 revision history, WS-4, and the March 14 CPSC investigation). The executed original version cannot be identified from this copy.
