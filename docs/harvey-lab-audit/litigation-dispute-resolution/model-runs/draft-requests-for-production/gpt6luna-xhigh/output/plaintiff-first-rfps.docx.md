PLAINTIFF BLACKTHORN MANUFACTURING, INC.’S FIRST REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION OF DOCUMENTS TO DEFENDANT RIDGELINE DISTRIBUTION PARTNERS, LLC

Pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 34, Plaintiff Blackthorn Manufacturing, Inc. (“Blackthorn”) requests that Defendant Ridgeline Distribution Partners, LLC (“Ridgeline”) produce the documents and electronically stored information (“ESI”) described below for inspection and copying within the time prescribed by Rule 34(b)(2)(A), at the offices of Caldwell, Harding & Pratt LLP, 1200 Superior Avenue, Suite 2400, Cleveland, Ohio 44114, or by secure electronic production as counsel may agree. These requests are continuing to the extent required by Rule 26(e).

# I. DEFINITIONS

**“Action”** means this action, Blackthorn Manufacturing, Inc. v. Ridgeline Distribution Partners, LLC, Case No. 1:24-cv-01837-JRK, pending in the United States District Court for the Northern District of Ohio.

**“Blackthorn”** means Plaintiff Blackthorn Manufacturing, Inc., including its officers, employees, and agents, but only to the extent a particular request seeks documents in Ridgeline’s possession, custody, or control.

**“Ridgeline” or “You”** means Defendant Ridgeline Distribution Partners, LLC and its officers, employees, agents, and representatives acting on its behalf, including relevant former employees whose Ridgeline business records remain in Ridgeline’s possession, custody, or control.

**“Apex”** means Apex Industrial Components, Inc., including its officers and employees, when referred to in documents or communications in Ridgeline’s possession, custody, or control.

**“EDA”** means the Exclusive Distribution Agreement between Blackthorn and Ridgeline effective January 1, 2018, including its exhibits and any amendments, addenda, renewals, modifications, waivers, or written or oral agreements alleged to supplement or vary it.

**“Southwest Territory”** means Texas, Louisiana, Oklahoma, New Mexico, and Colorado, as defined in the EDA.

**“Blackthorn Products”** means the products covered by the EDA, including the product categories identified in EDA Exhibit B.

**“Competing Products”** means products within the definition in EDA § 1.4, including precision-machined industrial valve components manufactured, assembled, or supplied by Apex or another entity other than Blackthorn that are designed for the same or substantially similar applications as Blackthorn Products.

**“Blackthorn Confidential Information”** means Confidential Information as defined in EDA § 1.5, including Blackthorn pricing data, customer information, ValvePrime materials, source code, pricing tier data, and historical pricing adjustment data.

**“ValvePrime Materials”** means ValvePrime v3.2 and any other version of the ValvePrime dynamic pricing engine, including source code, repositories, algorithms, models, configuration files, pricing tier matrices, historical pricing data, and any copies, extracts, summaries, analyses, or derivatives of those materials.

**“Identified Accounts”** means the 47 Blackthorn customer accounts described in Complaint ¶¶ 45–46 and its referenced Exhibit E, including account names or unique identifiers furnished by Blackthorn in its Rule 26 disclosures or a written account schedule in this Action.

**“Communication”** means the transmission or exchange of information by any means, including email, text message, instant message, collaboration platforms (including Slack or Microsoft Teams), telephone or voicemail, letters, and in-person meeting notes or recordings; it includes attachments and associated metadata.

**“Document”** has the broad meaning in Rule 34(a) and includes writings, records, photographs, recordings, drawings, charts, and data compilations, whether paper or electronic, including drafts and non-identical copies.

**“ESI”** means information stored in any electronic or digital medium, including email, messages, files, source code, databases, spreadsheets, CRM data, access and audit logs, metadata, cloud data, backups, and information on computers, mobile devices, and portable media.

**“Present”** means the date these Requests are served.

# II. INSTRUCTIONS

**1. Scope and possession, custody, or control.** Produce all responsive, nonprivileged documents and ESI in Ridgeline’s possession, custody, or control, including responsive materials maintained by its employees, agents, service providers, or former employees on its behalf. This includes responsive business records on personal devices or accounts used to conduct Ridgeline business, limited to such business records.

**2. Separate responses; organization.** Respond separately to each Request. Produce documents as kept in the usual course of business or organize and label them to correspond to the Request(s) to which they respond, as required by Rule 34(b)(2)(E)(i). A document responsive to more than one Request need not be produced more than once if its production identifies each responsive Request.

**3. ESI custodians and date ranges.** Each Request seeking ESI identifies the relevant custodians or record-system personnel and time period. Those limitations apply to ESI searches for that Request. For Ridgeline account executives reporting to Victor Escobar, include the Southwest Territory account executives who received or implemented the relevant directions, whether or not their names are presently known to Blackthorn. The requests do not seek unrelated personal material.

**4. Form of production; native format.** Under Rule 34(b)(1)(C), the Case Management Order § IV.C, and the Stipulated Protective Order §§ 8.1–8.4, Blackthorn specifies the following forms. Produce responsive email in native MSG, PST, or functionally equivalent format that preserves full headers, attachments, threading, and parent-child relationships, accompanied by load files. Produce ValvePrime source-code and other native electronic files alleged to have been downloaded, copied, or transferred; spreadsheets; databases or structured data; system, portal, access, and audit logs; and ESI whose metadata or formulas are material in native format with associated metadata intact. For RFP No. 21, produce the requested tangible devices or media for inspection and testing under a protocol agreed by the parties or ordered by the Court. Produce other ESI in the Case Management Order’s default single-page TIFF format with extracted text and load files, unless the parties agree otherwise. Do not flatten or convert a specified native-format item in a way that removes or obscures its metadata.

**5. Metadata and relationships.** For native productions, preserve and provide available original metadata, including custodian, author, creation and last-modified dates, sent/received dates, sender and recipients (To, CC, BCC), subject, file name and original path, extension, file size, and hash value, plus access, download, copy, transfer, and device identifiers where maintained. Maintain parent-child relationships for email and attachments. For TIFF productions, provide the metadata fields required by the Case Management Order and Protective Order.

**6. Privilege and protection.** If any responsive material is withheld on a claim of privilege or work-product protection, identify it in a manner sufficient to comply with Rule 26(b)(5)(A) and the Case Management Order. These Requests do not seek privileged communications or attorney work product. They do seek nonprivileged facts concerning whether, when, and how a preservation hold was issued and implemented, as specifically stated in RFP No. 32.

**7. Inaccessible, lost, or unavailable material.** If responsive ESI is withheld as not reasonably accessible, identify the source and state the basis for that position as required by Rule 26(b)(2)(B). If responsive material once existed but is no longer in Ridgeline’s possession, custody, or control, identify its general nature, the custodian and source, when and how it became unavailable, and any steps taken to preserve, restore, or retrieve it, subject to applicable privilege.

**8. Confidentiality.** Responsive material may be designated and handled under the Stipulated Protective Order. A confidentiality designation does not excuse production.

**9. Supplementation.** Supplement responses as required by Rule 26(e).
