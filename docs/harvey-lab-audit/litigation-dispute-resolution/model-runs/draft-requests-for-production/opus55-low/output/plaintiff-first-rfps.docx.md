**INSTRUCTIONS**

1\. **Possession, custody, or control.** These Requests call for all responsive Documents and things in Ridgeline’s possession, custody, or control, including Documents held by Ridgeline’s agents, attorneys, accountants, and IT or cloud service providers, and Documents authored, sent, received, or maintained by current or former Ridgeline employees (including Amanda Briggs) that remain on Ridgeline’s systems, archives, or backups. Documents on personally owned devices or accounts that Ridgeline personnel used to conduct Ridgeline business are within Ridgeline’s control. These Requests do not seek Apex’s internal Documents that are not within Ridgeline’s possession, custody, or control.

2\. **Custodians and date ranges.** In accordance with Section IV.A of the CMO and the Court’s Standing Order on Civil Cases, each Request that seeks ESI identifies the custodians and date range applicable to that Request. Where a Request identifies non-custodial sources (e.g., accounting, ERP, CRM, logging, or file-share systems), Ridgeline shall search those sources in addition to the identified custodians. Unless a Request states otherwise, the date range for each Request includes Documents created, sent, received, or modified within the stated period. Blackthorn will meet and confer regarding search terms and any technology-assisted review protocol pursuant to Section IV.B of the CMO, and requests that Ridgeline disclose its proposed search methodology before applying it.

3\. **Form of production — native ESI (Fed. R. Civ. P. 34(b)(1)(C)).** Pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 34(b)(1)(C), Section IV.C of the CMO, and Sections 8.2 through 8.4 of the Protective Order, Blackthorn specifies that the following categories of ESI be produced in **native format with all system and application metadata intact** (including file-system created, modified, and last-accessed timestamps, original file path, custodian, author, and MD5 or SHA-1 hash value), accompanied by a load file containing the metadata fields listed in Section 8.1(a) of the Protective Order and a Bates-numbered slip sheet for each native file: (a) the Downloaded Materials and any other files containing or derived from ValvePrime or Blackthorn pricing data; (b) source code, scripts, and configuration files; (c) system, network, firewall, proxy, VPN, endpoint, authentication, USB/removable-media, and application access logs, together with sufficient information to identify the time zone of each timestamp; (d) files stored on or transferred to or from USB drives, external storage, cloud storage, or personal devices; (e) spreadsheets and other files in which formulas, hidden content, or embedded data are material; (f) email, which shall be produced in native format (.msg, .eml, or .pst) or, at a minimum, as TIFF images with extracted text and a load file preserving full header information and parent–attachment (family) relationships, with native production of any email or attachment on request; and (g) text, chat, and instant messages, which shall be produced in a format that preserves sender, all recipients, timestamps, conversation threading, and attachments (e.g., Relativity Short Message Format or a native forensic export). This specification is made because file metadata is itself probative evidence of access, copying, modification, and transmission in this trade secret action, as the parties acknowledged in Section 8.3 of the Protective Order. Ridgeline shall not convert any ESI from native format to an image format in a manner that strips or obscures metadata (Protective Order § 8.2). If Ridgeline objects to any specified form, it must state the objection and the form it intends to use, as required by Fed. R. Civ. P. 34(b)(2)(D).

4\. **Form of production — other Documents and structured data.** All other ESI may be produced in the default format set forth in Section IV.C of the CMO and Section 8.1 of the Protective Order (single-page TIFF, extracted text, and metadata load files). Structured data (including accounting, ERP, sales, inventory, and CRM data) shall be produced as native database exports or delimited/Excel reports containing all responsive fields, together with a data dictionary identifying each field; Blackthorn requests the meet-and-confer regarding database productions contemplated by Section 8.1(c) of the Protective Order. Hard-copy Documents shall be scanned and produced as TIFF images with logical unitization and an indication of the file or location from which they came.

5\. **Organization and de-duplication.** Documents shall be produced as they are kept in the usual course of business or organized and labeled to correspond to the Requests, as required by Fed. R. Civ. P. 34(b)(2)(E)(i). Ridgeline may de-duplicate globally using hash values, provided that it identifies every custodian from whose files each de-duplicated Document was collected (Protective Order § 8.5).

6\. **Objections.** For each Request, Ridgeline shall state whether it will produce the requested Documents or state with specificity the grounds for any objection, and shall state whether any responsive materials are being withheld on the basis of that objection, as required by Fed. R. Civ. P. 34(b)(2)(B)–(C). An objection to part of a Request must specify the part, and Ridgeline shall produce all Documents responsive to the remainder.

7\. **Privilege.** If any Document is withheld or redacted on the basis of privilege or work-product protection, Ridgeline shall provide a privilege log complying with Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(b)(5)(A) and Section IV.D of the CMO, identifying for each Document its date, author, all recipients, general subject matter, and the privilege asserted. Redactions shall be clearly marked and logged (Protective Order § 8.6). Privilege logs shall be served on a rolling basis and in any event no later than the February 12, 2025 deadline set by the CMO.

8\. **Lost or destroyed Documents.** If any responsive Document or thing has been lost, discarded, destroyed, deleted, overwritten, wiped, or otherwise disposed of, Ridgeline shall identify the Document or thing, the date and circumstances of its disposition, the Person(s) responsible, and any policy or instruction under which it occurred.

9\. **Tangible things and inspection.** Where a Request seeks tangible things or devices under Fed. R. Civ. P. 34(a)(1)(A)–(B), Blackthorn proposes that production be accomplished by forensic imaging performed by a mutually agreed neutral forensic examiner under a stipulated forensic protocol that protects privileged and irrelevant personal information, and requests that the parties meet and confer regarding that protocol within fourteen (14) days of service. Pending inspection, each such device shall be preserved in its current state and shall not be used, altered, reimaged, or wiped.

10\. **Confidentiality.** Confidentiality concerns are not a basis to withhold production. Ridgeline may designate produced materials under the Protective Order, subject to its good-faith requirement (§ 3.3).

11\. **No admission.** The use of any defined term, or the description of any allegation, is not an admission by Blackthorn of any fact or characterization, and does not adopt any contention of Ridgeline.

12\. **Continuing obligation.** These Requests are continuing. Ridgeline shall timely supplement its responses and production pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(e).
