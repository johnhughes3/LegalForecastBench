# 1. Executive Summary

We checked every one of the 139 fee entries and 22 expense lines in the invoice against the Billing Guidelines, the Engagement Letter, the Exhibit A rate schedule, the Exhibit B expert approvals, the Prior Approval Log and the transmittal email. The invoice has serious problems at three levels:

- **Internal arithmetic does not reconcile.** The fee line items add up to \$269,554.00 (743.8 hours). The invoice states fees of \$242,687.00 (671.8 hours). The expense lines add up to \$244,652.14, not the stated \$244,642.14. Together the line items total \$514,206.14, which is \$26,877.00 more than the stated invoice total. Seven of the eight timekeeper subtotals are understated. Because the invoice does not reconcile, it cannot be processed as submitted.

- **Charges billed without the required approval, or that can never be billed.** Several charges required prior approval and have none: an unapproved summer associate; contract-attorney time billed after the approval expired; San Francisco travel outside the Litigation Forum; a \$48,250 e-discovery vendor; local counsel; and firm overhead charges that the Guidelines prohibit outright.

- **Invoice-level and format failures.** The invoice exceeds \$350,000 but no written explanation was provided (§10.1). Cumulative 2024 spend has passed the 75% budget threshold (\$2,100,000) with no notification. Every entry uses UTBMS task codes (L510–L550) that are not on the approved list in Appendix A. 138 of 139 narratives are shorter than the 30-word minimum.

**D-20 — LEDES/UTBMS coding (§4.4, §8.1, Appendices A–B).**

- **Task codes.** Appendix A approves litigation codes L110–L190 only. All 139 fee entries use L510, L520, L530, L540 or L550, which are not on that list. In the standard UTBMS litigation code set, the L500 series is the Appeal phase, which misdescribes the work. For example, discovery work should be L130 and expert work L140.

- **Activity codes.** Activity codes appear to be misapplied against the standard UTBMS set. Deposition and inspection attendance is coded A104 (Review/Analyze), not A109 (Appear/Attend). Internal conferences are coded A106 (Communicate with client). Research is coded A101 (Plan and prepare), not A102. Travel is coded A107.

- **Expense codes.** Measured against the standard UTBMS expense codes, most codes do not fit the charge. Airfare is coded E101 (which is copying), transcripts E107, Westlaw E108, experts E112 and local counsel E115. The standard codes would be E110 for out-of-town travel, E115 for transcripts, E106 for online research, E119 for experts and E122 for local counsel. Appendix A does not reproduce expense codes, so this item should be confirmed with VIH's e-billing administrator.

- **Field set and narratives.** The LEDES export has no TIMEKEEPER_NAME field, which Appendix B requires. Its narratives are shortened versions of the Excel narratives (for example, entry 2 drops the recommendations, and entry 32 drops the list of documents reviewed). The two versions of the invoice are therefore not the same. The email says a separate LEDES 1998B flat file was attached, but only the Excel export was in the materials reviewed. VIH should confirm what it actually received.
