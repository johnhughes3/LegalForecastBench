# Detailed issues and required revisions

## 1. Engagement does not incorporate the OCG or identify Pinnacle matter number

**HIGH \| Documents:** Engagement letter §§1, 19–20; OCG §§I.1, II.1

**Guideline:** The engagement letter must expressly reference and incorporate the OCG and state HB&C agrees to comply with it. It must identify the matter name and Pinnacle matter number.

**Assessment:** The letter is silent on the OCG, has no Pinnacle matter number, and does not include the required express compliance statement. Its entire-agreement clause says the letter and exhibits supersede prior understandings; its amendment clause requires signatures by both parties. The OCG supersedes conflicting terms and allows OCG amendments on 30 days’ written notice, with waivers/modifications requiring a separate GC-signed writing. The draft should not imply that bilateral amendment language limits those rules.

**Required action:** Revise the opening and entire-agreement provisions to state that the January 2024 Pinnacle OCG is incorporated in full, controls in case of conflict, and is accepted by HB&C; add the Pinnacle matter number. Keep any waiver separate, specific, and signed by the GC.

## 2. Material conflict disclosed; no compliant clearance or resolution

**CRITICAL \| Documents:** Morrow email Oct. 3; Alcott email Oct. 4; engagement letter §11; OCG §II.2

**Guideline:** Before work, HB&C must provide a comprehensive written conflicts clearance covering adverse parties/affiliates, entities with ≥5% financial interests, personal/business relationships, and other potential conflicts. A specific conflict requires detailed disclosure and GC’s informed written consent; blanket advance waivers are prohibited.

**Assessment:** Morrow disclosed HB&C’s current representation of MedBridge in a reimbursement dispute and MedBridge’s approximately 8% equity stake in NovaTech. The OCG defines a ≥5% equity stake as a material financial interest. The fact that MedBridge is a passive investor or the other matter is unrelated does not remove it from the OCG’s stated disclosure/consent test. Alcott’s follow-up says the check covered NovaTech, known principals, and Prescott Lane, but omits MedBridge and material investors; it is not a clearance letter and is materially incomplete on the known issue. The draft’s blanket waiver of future unrelated adverse representations is expressly unacceptable.

**Required action:** Treat this as a stop issue. Require a written, fact-complete disclosure, including scope/status of MedBridge work, confidential information access, relationships and potential adversity, plus a conflicts-team certification covering the firm and all expected personnel. Obtain a separate, matter-specific GC consent only after evaluating the full facts and applicable ethics rules. Strike the blanket waiver. If consent is unavailable or the conflict is nonconsentable, do not retain HB&C.

**Guideline:** Minimum coverage is \$25m per occurrence and \$50m aggregate; provide proof with conflicts clearance, confirm A.M. Best A- or better and no relevant exclusions, and update annually.

## 5. Proposed rates exceed OCG ceilings; escalation and blended option are noncompliant

**Guideline:** The OCG caps rates by role, fixes rates for the engagement-start calendar year, requires prior written GC approval for any later increase, and caps approved blended rates at \$625/hour.

Every currently stated core-team rate exceeds the maximum. The engagement letter’s fee-election checkboxes are blank, so it also does not establish whether standard or blended pricing is elected. Based on the 1,880 core-team hours in the staffing plan, the current-rate estimate is \$1,141,500; applying OCG caps to those same hours yields \$996,500, a \$145,000 reduction before any other changes. ## 6. Invoice mechanics do not meet LEDES, timing, narrative, or dispute requirements

**HIGH \| Documents:** Engagement letter §5; OCG §§III.4–5, XIII.1

**Guideline:** Invoices must be LEDES 1998B through Pinnacle’s platform, submitted within 45 days after month-end, with six-word minimum task descriptions, no block billing, date/timekeeper/rate, expense backup over \$250, matter number, and cumulative budget-to-actual. **Assessment:** The letter permits submission within 60 days, requires only general service descriptions, has no LEDES/platform, six-word, no-block-billing, receipt/documentation, matter-number, or budget-to-actual terms, and starts payment/1.5% monthly interest from invoice date. It asks Pinnacle to raise concerns within 30 days, potentially truncating the OCG’s 60-day dispute period. Interest could also be asserted on disputed/unapproved amounts.

**Required action:** Replace §5 with OCG-compliant terms: LEDES 1998B/platform, 45-day submission, required narrative and supporting detail, payment only for approved compliant invoices within 30 days of receipt/approval, undisputed portions paid while disputed sums follow OCG procedures, and preserve the full 60-day dispute period. Delete interest on disputed/unapproved charges and any deemed waiver after 30 days.
