## Preliminary Statement

Ridgeline Therapeutics, Inc. ("Ridgeline") submits this log pursuant to Section IV.B.2(iv) of the Stipulated Confidentiality and Clawback Order entered February 28, 2024 (the "Order") and Instruction C of Grand Jury Subpoena No. GJ-2024-00417. Each entry below identifies a document, or portion of a document, produced in Production 3 (June 10, 2024) that is protected by the attorney-client privilege and/or the work product doctrine and was produced inadvertently as a result of a software defect in the e-discovery vendor's email-threading propagation script (NB-RelScript-Thread-v4.2.1), which overwrote privilege designations that Ridgeline's reviewing attorneys had applied.

Production and non-waiver are governed by Fed. R. Evid. 502(d) and Section IV of the Order. Ridgeline elects that the Government **return or destroy** all copies of the documents identified below (including any copies in review databases, work files, or in the possession of agents) and certify compliance within five (5) business days under Section IV.C.1. Where a document is only partially privileged, Ridgeline will provide a replacement image bearing the same Bates numbers with the privileged portions redacted and marked "REDACTED — PRIVILEGED."

Abbreviations: **AC** = attorney-client privilege; **WP** = attorney work product (opinion unless noted); **CI** = common-interest doctrine; **Esq.** denotes an attorney. Ridgeline personnel: Priya Nagarajan, Esq. (General Counsel); Thomas Viklund, Esq. (Deputy General Counsel); Dr. Kevin Lassiter (VP, Medical Affairs); Raymond Ochoa (VP, Regulatory Affairs); Sandra Mullins (Director, Sales Training); Janet Correa (then-VP, Commercial Operations); Dr. Marcus Ashworth (CEO); Helen Pak-Morrison (Independent Director; Chair, Audit Committee). Outside counsel: Andrew Metcalf, Esq. (Kendrick Sable LLP, counsel to J. Correa); Rachel Greenwald, Esq. (Waverly Stone LLP, counsel to the Audit Committee).
