# **Executive Snapshot**

- Claims / counterclaim: Harborview pleads breach of contract (exclusivity and termination), breach of the implied covenant, fraud/intentional misrepresentation, and tortious interference; Greenleaf denies liability and counterclaims for the Year 3 minimum-purchase shortfall (\$1.2 million).

- Central chronology: Cascade shipments began in June 2022; Greenleaf leadership directed targeted QA screening in September 2022; Greenleaf cited an incomplete Year 3 purchasing period in a January 10, 2023 breach notice; it issued termination February 15, effective March 17; Harborview disputes validity and says no timely non-renewal notice was sent.

- Potentially powerful record: the executed EDA; Greenleaf’s pleading admission of certain Cascade shipments; the Holcomb deposition summary’s account of an approximately \$1.9M Oregon shipment admission (verify transcript and invoices); contemporaneous Holcomb/Stanton/Fong emails; and the notices/response.

- Critical caution: QA counts, denominators, lot identities, values, and standards do not reconcile across the QA spreadsheet, expert reports, and email/deposition summaries. Do not present Dr. Buckley’s statistical or lot-by-lot conclusions as established until the underlying dataset is reconciled.

- Procedural posture: the June 3, 2024 scheduling order sets dispositive motions by March 1, 2025 and trial for June 16, 2025. No later docket activity or amended order was supplied; confirm the live schedule before using this calendar.

## **Case Frame & Contract Provisions to Keep in View**

- EDA (effective March 15, 2020): initial term through March 14, 2023; automatic one-year renewal unless written non-renewal notice at least 90 days before expiration; Section 3.1 grants Harborview exclusive distribution rights to all Greenleaf Products in Oregon, Washington, Idaho, and Montana.

- Purchase minimums: Year 1 \$4.0M; Year 2 \$5.5M; Year 3 \$7.0M; each renewal year \$7.5M. Sections 5.2–5.3 measure the commitment over the full Purchase Year. Commission: 18% of defined Net Wholesale Revenue under Section 6.1.

- Termination: Section 9.2 requires written notice specifying the breach and a 30-day cure period; termination is available only for an actual material breach remaining uncured, and a notice before an actual breach or before the cure period ends is stated to have no force or effect. Section 9.3 governs auto-renewal.

- Other potentially material terms: §§5.1, 7.1–7.2 (order fulfillment and quality), 1.11 (Quality Standards may be amended in reasonable discretion upon written notice), Exhibit B (contract quality thresholds), §§12.1–12.2 (consequential-damage exclusion/liability cap, with express carve-outs including Article 3 exclusivity), §14.4 (notice effective upon actual receipt), §14.5 (written waiver), and §14.8 (Oregon law/forum).
