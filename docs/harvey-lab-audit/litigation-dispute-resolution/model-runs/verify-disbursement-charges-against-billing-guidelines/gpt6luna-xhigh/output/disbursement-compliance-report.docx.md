# 4. Invoice reconciliation and administration

- The 47 disbursement amounts sum to \$145,404.30; the Summary sheet reports \$86,742.19. The \$58,662.11 difference must be reconciled by HWK and a corrected invoice issued under Guidelines §6.7. The implied invoice total using the listed disbursements and stated professional fees is \$332,824.30 before reductions—not the stated \$274,162.19.

- The invoice’s stated “Net 30 days” conflicts with the engagement letter and Guidelines, which provide payment within 45 days of receipt of a compliant invoice. Correct the payment-term field; this is not a disbursement reduction.

- Actual-cost pass-throughs (experts, consultants, technology, vendors, travel, court costs, and printing) remain subject to original vendor invoices/receipts and verification that no markup or handling charge was added. Guidelines §5.1 and the engagement letter §6 require actual cost and supporting records.

# 5. Recommended next steps

- Return the invoice for reconciliation of the \$58,662.11 schedule-to-summary variance before processing payment.

- Apply the \$17,358.30 recommended face-of-record reductions, subject to the stated e-discovery scope clarification; request a revised, arithmetically accurate invoice.

- Place the \$30,190 of unapproved charges on hold and request written retroactive approvals. If denied, reject those charges; obtain General Counsel approval before reductions totaling \$25,000 or more.

- Request missing coach-fare, rental-class, courier, meal, and actual-cost support described above. Do not treat undocumented actual-cost claims as verified.

- Re-review the revised invoice totals and ensure every disbursement is coded and described sufficiently under the Guidelines before approval.
