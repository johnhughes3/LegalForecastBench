# 1. Executive Summary

- The invoice arithmetic is wrong. The disbursement line items add up to \$145,404.30, but the Summary sheet shows \$86,742.19, an understatement of \$58,662.11. Professional fee lines add up to \$161,698.50, while the Summary shows \$187,420.00, an overstatement of \$25,721.50. Brooks, the Hartsfield overage, and the Relativity overage plus the predictive coding module. Together these account for \$35,390.00.

# 4. Items Requiring Documentation or Clarification (No Reduction Quantified)

- Line 14 – Rental car, full-size sedan (\$127.00). §5.2 allows only mid-size vehicles. Reimburse at the documented mid-size rate for the same day and location, and ask HWK to show that a mid-size car was unavailable. The difference should be deducted once known.

- Lines 1, 2, 11 – Airfare. The amounts are consistent with the approved coach estimate (\$450–\$500). Confirm by receipt that the D.C. flight (Line 11) was coach class.

- Lines 17, 18 – Copyland Express outside printing (\$3,475 and \$2,180). Under §5.3 these are billed at actual cost and need the vendor invoice. Line 17 is described as 'trial exhibit binders' even though trial is set for February 2026. Confirm the purpose.

- Line 28 – \$52 'filing fee, motion to compel.' W.D.N.C. does not normally charge a fee to file a motion through CM/ECF. Request the court receipt.

- Line 32 – FedEx overnight of a 'class certification motion to court' (5/1). Filings are made via CM/ECF, and HWK's class certification opposition was not filed until 5/23. Request an explanation (§5.6 time-sensitivity).

- Line 34 – Charlotte local courier of mediation materials to 'Judge Royce's office.' The Firm's own entries place the mediator in Washington, D.C. Confirm the destination and contents.

- Lines 26, 23, 27 – TechForensic (\$5,100) matches its approval. Redmond (\$18,750) is within its \$20,000 cap. Allowed amounts for all expert and consultant lines depend on receiving original invoices showing no markup (§5.4).

- Travel meal lines (5, 6, 7, 13) use generic 'Meals' descriptions that do not meet §5.1/§5.2. Request itemized receipts and confirm alcohol was excluded. If alcohol was not deducted, §5.2 allows rejecting the whole meal charge.

# 6. Other Observations (Outside Disbursement Scope)

- Payment terms: the invoice says 'Net 30 days' (due July 3, 2025). The Engagement Letter §5 and Guidelines §8.5 provide net 45 days from receipt of a compliant invoice. The 45-day clock should not start until a corrected invoice arrives.

- Rates: Taro Nakamura is billed at \$575/hr, but Exhibit A sets \$625/hr. This underbilling favors Pinnacle, but HWK should confirm it will not try to recover the difference later.

- Professional fees: several time entries relate to disallowed items. These include coordinating GraphicWorks, DataScan and contract attorney review, the summer-associate dinner (Line 32), the telephonic hearing described as 'attend' (Line 62), and 'conference with billing department' and 'compile billing entries' (Lines 73 and 75), which are non-billable administrative work under §4.6. Consider these in the separate fee review.

- The travel time entries (Lines 5, 9, 26, 29, 36) appear to be billed at full rate, not the 50% required by §4.5 and Engagement Letter §4. Recommend a separate fee-side adjustment.
