Do not approve the invoice as submitted. The record supports material reductions for unapproved staffing, a contract-attorney rate above the approved pass-through rate, excessive staffing at the May 8 internal strategy call and Dr. Liu deposition, block billing, and work billed at an inappropriate level. # 8. Additional compliance points

- The June 5 invoice is timely for a May billing period (guideline deadline: June 15).

- The invoice lists the litigation case number but no Pinnacle matter number; add the Company matter number required by §9.1(a).

- Invoice terms state net 30 (due July 5), while §9.3 provides review/payment processing within 45 days of receipt absent a controlling engagement-letter modification. Confirm the governing term and correct the invoice; this is not a fee reduction.

- All displayed time entries are whole- or half-hour values. The Guidelines require timekeeping increments no greater than 0.1 hour. The displayed numbers alone do not prove how the underlying time was rounded; request contemporaneous tenths-based records and correct any entries rounded to larger increments. No separate mechanical adjustment is calculated without those records.

- Resubmission of split block-billed entries is permitted within 15 days after notice under §5.4. Request the corrected detail and contemporaneous time records promptly.
