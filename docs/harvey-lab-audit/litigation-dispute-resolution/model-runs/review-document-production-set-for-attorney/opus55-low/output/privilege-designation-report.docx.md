**Designation: Not Privileged — Produce, subject to NB final sign-off (Protocol §8(b)).** Responsive to RFPs 2, 3, 9 and 16.

## Doc 10 — Litigation Hold Notice (Oct. 11, 2024)

**Designation: Privileged — Withhold with Caveats.** This departs from the preliminary “Not Privileged — Produce” call in the Review Notes tab. It is responsive to RFP 12.

- **Why it is privileged.** It is a confidential communication from the GC to 14 employees who each had a need to receive the instructions. It gives legal direction on preservation obligations, the scope of the hold and the consequences of non-compliance. Courts generally treat hold notices as privileged or work product.
- **What remains discoverable.** The *facts* of preservation are discoverable: the date, the custodians, and the categories and types of material. The notices themselves can be ordered produced if spoliation becomes an issue.
- **Recommendation.** Withhold and log the notice, but disclose the date, the custodian list and the preservation categories in the RFP 12 response. That keeps privilege intact while meeting plaintiff’s legitimate need.
- **Voluntary production is an option.** Producing the notice would carry little waiver risk, since it contains no legal analysis. CF/NB may choose that route to show good faith.
- **Preservation gaps to fix regardless of the privilege call:**
  - The 23-member Slack channel includes many non-custodians, such as sales reps, the marketing intern and the warehouse team.
  - Dr. Moritani is covered only as a category, not as a custodian.
  - The notice uses a different email domain (greenleafcpg.com) from the company’s email (greenleafconsumer.com).
