# 1. Executive Summary

I checked Hargrove & Linden's July 2024 invoice for the Cascade litigation against four sources: TerraVerde's Outside Counsel Billing Guidelines (rev. Nov. 2023), which are incorporated into the January 22, 2024 engagement letter; the engagement letter; your June 28, 2024 email to Diane Hargrove about research billing; and the firm's June 2024 Cascade invoice and July 2024 DOE-matter invoice. The invoice has the problems you warned about in your June 28 email, and some new ones. The main issues are:

- **Unsupported fees.** The Summary sheet bills \$349,552.50 in gross professional fees. The 160 itemized Fee Detail entries add up to only \$243,080.50 (565.9 hours). That leaves \$106,472.00 with no supporting time entries. The research totals also disagree: 142.3 hours on the Summary versus 137.6 hours on the Discount sheet and in the RES-coded entries.

- **Research cap breach and discount error.** The "15%" research discount of \$7,732.00 is really only 14.29%; 15% would be \$8,115.68. Even after that discount, the firm itself reports research at 13.56% of fees, which is over the 12% cap in §6.2. No written approval was requested, although your email specifically asked for it. Measured against the fees that are actually itemized, gross research is 22.3% of fees (\$54,989.50 including a reclassified block-billed entry, divided by \$243,080.50).

- **Specific research violations:** Partner research billed at full partner rates (§6.3); the same issues researched by multiple timekeepers in the same month (§6.4); "background"/"get up to speed" research (§§4.3, 6.4); the same MTCA research billed to both the Cascade and DOE matters (§6.6); and Westlaw charges passed through as a disbursement (§9.3).

- **Other violations:** Travel time billed at 100% instead of 50%; three attorneys at a deposition; a senior associate preparing deposition summaries; firm-internal administrative time; and an \$18,500 expert charge, plus travel expenses, with no documented pre-approval.

Recommendation: pay \$222,732.10 now. Withhold \$106,472.00 in unsupported fees and \$24,250.00 in disbursements until the firm provides documentation. Disallow \$37,383.50 in specific fee and disbursement charges. The table below shows how these figures add up.

# 6. Invoice Integrity Issues

- **Unitemized fees.** Gross fees of \$349,552.50 versus \$243,080.50 itemized. The \$106,472.00 difference is not supported by any time entry, which does not meet §5.1(e). Withhold it and require complete entries before any payment of this amount.

- **Hour mismatch.** 142.3 research hours (Summary) versus 137.6 hours (Discount sheet and entries).

- **Discount arithmetic.** \$7,732.00 is presented as 15% of \$54,104.50, which is actually \$8,115.68.

- **Cap denominator.** The Summary calculates research as a percentage of \$341,820.50 in net fees. That inflates the denominator with unsupported fees and understates the cap problem.

- **Reply brief duplication.** The reply brief appears to have been filed twice (L34–36 on 7/11: "approve for filing… file with court"; L61–63 on 7/19: "final review… E-file"). Duplicate final reviews by Hargrove (L34, L62) and Briggs (L35) should be explained. They are flagged, not adjusted, pending the firm's explanation.
