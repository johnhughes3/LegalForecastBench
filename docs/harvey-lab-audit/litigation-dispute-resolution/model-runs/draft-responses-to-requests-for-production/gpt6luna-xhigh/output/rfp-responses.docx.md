# PRELIMINARY STATEMENT

Redfield has undertaken collection and review efforts but has not completed every targeted supplemental collection described below. The responses state the scope Redfield proposes to search and produce, identify known limitations, and preserve the obligation to supplement. “Will produce” means responsive, nonprivileged documents within Redfield’s possession, custody, or control, subject to the stated scope and any protective-order safeguards. Redfield does not represent that its collection is complete until the outstanding custodian, source, and factual checks noted in these responses are finished.

# GENERAL OBJECTIONS AND PRODUCTION CONVENTIONS

**1. Possession, custody, or control.** Redfield will search sources within its possession, custody, or control after reasonable inquiry. It objects to any instruction imposing a broader obligation to obtain materials from every affiliate, former employee, attorney, consultant, or other third party without regard to Redfield’s legal right or practical ability to obtain them.

**2. Relevance and proportionality.** Redfield objects to requests only to the extent stated in the individual responses where their breadth, subject matter, custodians, or time period is not proportional to the claims and defenses. The specific response identifies the proposed narrowing and whether material is withheld.

**3. Privilege and work product.** Redfield will not produce communications protected by attorney-client privilege, attorney work product, or another applicable protection. Any withholding will be document-specific, and Redfield will serve a corrected, sufficiently particularized privilege log. Nonprivileged factual material and reasonably segregable portions will be produced.

**4. Confidential business information.** Redfield objects to unrestricted disclosure of competitively sensitive pricing, costs, customer, and technical information. Confidentiality alone is not a basis to refuse otherwise discoverable material. Redfield proposes an agreed protective order, including an attorneys’-eyes-only tier for trade secrets and highly sensitive cost or engineering information, and states in each response where production is held pending that protection.

**5. ESI and production format.** Redfield will produce ESI in the parties’ previously used TIFF, extracted-text, and load-file format, with available agreed metadata, and will use native format where functionality or a reasonable agreement requires it. Redfield objects to any instruction requiring creation of information or metadata not maintained in the ordinary course or restoration of sources not reasonably accessible without a proportionality assessment. The parties should confer on any additional fields or native productions.
