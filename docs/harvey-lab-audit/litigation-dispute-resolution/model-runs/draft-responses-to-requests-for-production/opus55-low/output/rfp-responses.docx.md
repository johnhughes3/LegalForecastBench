**DEFENDANT REDFIELD MANUFACTURING, INC.’S RESPONSES AND OBJECTIONS TO PLAINTIFF’S FIRST SET OF REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION OF DOCUMENTS (NOS. 1–25)**

Defendant Redfield Manufacturing, Inc. (“Redfield”), through its attorneys, Blackwell, Trent & Gallagher LLP, responds and objects under MCR 2.302 and MCR 2.310 to Plaintiff Lakeshore Supply Partners, LLC’s (“Lakeshore”) First Set of Requests for Production of Documents (Nos. 1–25), served December 2, 2024 (the “Requests”).

# I. PRELIMINARY STATEMENT

These responses are based on Redfield’s reasonable investigation to date. Discovery is ongoing and document review is not complete. Redfield will supplement these responses as required by MCR 2.302(E). When a response below says Redfield “will produce” documents, it means Redfield will produce responsive, non-privileged documents within its possession, custody, or control that it locates through the reasonable search described in Section III. It does not mean Redfield represents that any such document exists.

Unless a response says otherwise, Redfield will produce documents on a rolling basis. Productions will begin within fourteen (14) days after service of these responses and will be substantially complete by \[date to be agreed with Plaintiff, proposed February 28, 2025\]. Documents designated Confidential or Attorneys’ Eyes Only will be produced within seven (7) days after the Court enters a stipulated protective order (see Section IV).

Redfield has already produced 1,247 documents, Bates-stamped REDFIELD-000001 through REDFIELD-001247, in its November 20, 2024 preliminary production. Where those documents respond to a Request, Redfield identifies them below by category and will provide a Bates cross-reference index with its first rolling production.

By letter dated \[\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\], 2024, Redfield notified Plaintiff’s counsel that the document Bates-stamped REDFIELD-000847 was produced inadvertently and is protected by the attorney-client privilege. Redfield requested that Plaintiff return, sequester, or destroy all copies, as MCR 2.302(B)(7) provides. That document is identified on Redfield’s privilege log. Redfield does not waive any privilege by its inadvertent production. Nothing in these responses is intended as, or should be construed as, such a waiver.

Redfield’s agreement to produce documents in response to a Request does not concede that the documents are relevant or admissible, and does not adopt any characterization contained in the Request.

# II. GENERAL OBJECTIONS

The following general objections are stated only to preserve them. Each response below independently states every objection Redfield relies on for that Request, and states whether any documents are being withheld on the basis of that objection. Redfield does not rely on any general objection to withhold documents unless the specific response repeats and explains it.

1\. **Supplementation.** Redfield reserves the right to supplement, amend, or correct these responses as its investigation, review, and discovery continue.

2\. **Privilege.** Redfield objects to producing information protected by the attorney-client privilege, the work-product doctrine (MCR 2.302(B)(3)), or any other applicable privilege or immunity. Privileged documents withheld from production are identified on Redfield’s privilege log, served with these responses. Redfield proposes that the parties agree that neither side needs to log communications exchanged after September 15, 2024 solely between a party and its outside litigation counsel, or work product created by outside litigation counsel after that date.

3\. **Relevance and Proportionality.** Redfield objects to Requests that seek information that is not relevant to any party’s claims or defenses, or that is not proportional to the needs of the case under MCR 2.302(B)(1). Where Redfield relies on this objection, the specific response explains why and proposes a narrower scope.

4\. **Definitions and Instructions.** Redfield objects to Definitions and Instructions that would impose obligations beyond those in the Michigan Court Rules. This includes Definition 2 (“You”), to the extent it reaches unrelated affiliates, “agents,” or persons “purporting to act” for Redfield; Definition 10 (“having any logical connection with”); and Instruction 1, which sets a default period beginning January 1, 2015. That date is six years before the Agreement was signed. Redfield also notes the following for clarity. Instruction 7 states that this action was commenced on October 7, 2024, but the Complaint was filed on October 1, 2024. Definition 7 cites Section 2.1 of the Agreement, but the Territory is defined in Section 2.2 and Exhibit B.

5\. **Equally Available Information.** Redfield objects to producing documents that Lakeshore itself sent or received, such as its own purchase orders and correspondence, when those documents are equally available to Lakeshore. Redfield will nonetheless produce such documents from its own files as stated below. It is not withholding documents on this ground alone.

6\. **Reservation of Additional Objections.** Redfield reserves the right to assert further objections that become apparent during its continuing review.

# III. SCOPE OF SEARCH AND STATEMENT REGARDING ESI AVAILABILITY

**Custodians and sources.** Redfield has collected documents and ESI from twelve custodians: Thomas Redfield III, Patricia Ng, David Kessler, Linda Chen, Robert Tanaka, Sandra Whitfield, James Morell, Angela Reeves, Victor Platt, Denise Hargrove, Brian Kowalski, and Karen Lowe. The sources collected are (a) Microsoft Exchange email from January 1, 2020 through December 1, 2024; (b) the Sales, Legal, Quality Assurance, Operations, Finance, and Executive folders of Redfield’s corporate file server; (c) forensic images of the local drives of eight custodians; (d) available Slack and Microsoft Teams data; and (e) hard-copy files kept in custodian offices at Redfield’s Grand Rapids headquarters. Redfield is also (i) collecting hard-copy operations and quality records at its Decatur, Alabama facility, (ii) extracting transactional data from its enterprise resource planning (ERP) system, and (iii) \[collecting business-related text messages between Mr. Kessler and Northpoint personnel from Mr. Kessler’s personal mobile device\]. Redfield will meet and confer about search terms and will disclose the terms it uses.

**Slack.** Redfield’s enterprise Slack workspace was configured with a company-wide retention setting that automatically deletes messages after 90 days. Redfield issued a litigation hold on September 16, 2024. That hold covered email, network drives, and local drives, but it did not suspend the Slack retention setting. The setting was suspended when a supplemental hold was issued on November 1, 2024. As a result, Slack messages dated before approximately August 3, 2024 were not available for collection. Redfield and its forensic vendor have attempted recovery through Redfield’s IT department and Slack enterprise support, and those efforts have been unsuccessful. Slack messages from approximately August 3, 2024 forward have been preserved and collected. Redfield’s Slack workspace was used for internal communication among Redfield employees. Redfield is not presently aware of Slack channels shared with Lakeshore or Northpoint. \[Confirm with B. Kowalski before service.\] Redfield is continuing to evaluate whether any residual Slack data can be recovered from custodian devices and will supplement this statement as required.

**Microsoft Teams.** Teams messages are subject to a one-year retention policy. Teams messages from approximately November 2023 forward have been preserved and collected. Teams messages from before that date are not available.

**Voicemail.** Redfield’s VoIP telephone system automatically deletes voicemail after 60 days. No voicemail recordings were preserved. Redfield therefore has no voicemail recordings to produce.

Redfield provides this statement to comply with Instruction 7 and in the interest of transparency. It is made without prejudice to Redfield’s position on when any duty to preserve arose, and without prejudice to any position either party may take about these circumstances. Redfield is willing to meet and confer about the information in this section, including whether other sources (such as email, Teams messages, and documents from Lakeshore or third parties) contain the substance of any unavailable communications.

# IV. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PROTECTIVE ORDER

Several Requests seek confidential commercial information. This includes pricing, discount, and margin data; distributor volumes; manufacturing processes; Bill of Materials data; and manufacturing costs. Much of it is also Confidential Information under Section 7.2 of the Agreement, which covers pricing, volume and sales data, product-development information, and business strategy. Some of it concerns Northpoint Distributors, Inc., a non-party competitor of Lakeshore. Redfield has sent Plaintiff’s counsel a proposed stipulated protective order. The proposed order has two confidentiality tiers (“Confidential” and “Attorneys’ Eyes Only”) and a non-waiver/clawback provision. Where a response below references this Section IV, Redfield will produce the affected documents with the appropriate designation promptly after the order is entered. If the parties cannot agree on the order, Redfield will move for one under MCR 2.302(C). Redfield is not withholding any document solely because it is confidential.

*Produce all Documents from 2015 to present concerning … Redfield’s sales, marketing, or distribution strategy for its commercial HVAC product line … within the Territory …*

**RESPONSE TO REQUEST NO. 3:**

**Temporal overbreadth.** The Agreement was negotiated in late 2020 and signed on January 15, 2021. The earliest alleged breach (the Northpoint relationship) arises in 2022–2023, and the Agreement expired on January 14, 2024. Distribution strategy from 2015 through mid-2020 predates any dealings under the Agreement by years and has no bearing on whether Redfield performed it. Redfield proposes a period of July 1, 2020 (six months before the Agreement was signed) through January 14, 2024. For documents that concern Lakeshore, Northpoint, or distribution of the Products in the Territory after the Agreement expired, Redfield proposes a period ending on the date of this response. Documents are being withheld in part on the basis of this objection: Redfield is not producing distribution-strategy documents dated before July 1, 2020. Responsive, non-privileged documents within the scope described above will be produced.

**Subject-matter overbreadth.** “Concerning, reflecting, or relating to” strategy, read with Definition 10, would reach every sales email and routine account communication about every commercial HVAC product. That material is not strategy and is covered by other, more specific Requests (e.g., Nos. 6, 8, 15). Redfield will produce strategic plans, market assessments, competitive analyses, business development plans, marketing presentations, board or executive presentations, and internal memoranda that address distribution of the Products, or of Industrial Series products, in the Territory. Documents are being withheld in part on the basis of this objection: routine transactional communications not otherwise responsive are excluded from this Request. Responsive, non-privileged documents within the scope described above will be produced.

**Confidential business information.** Strategic plans and competitive analyses are confidential commercial information and fall within Agreement Section 7.2(d). Redfield will produce them under the protective order described in Section IV. No documents are being withheld on the basis of this objection.

Subject to these objections, Redfield will produce the documents described above for the period July 1, 2020 through January 14, 2024. It will also produce documents after January 14, 2024 that concern Lakeshore, Northpoint, or distribution of the Products in the Territory. Redfield will include documents addressing how Industrial Series products relate to, or are classified relative to, the Commercial HVAC Product Line defined in the Agreement.

Redfield will produce the categories of communications identified in (a) through (c) above.

## REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 14:

*Produce all Documents reflecting the design specifications, engineering drawings, Bill of Materials, manufacturing processes, manufacturing costs, and quality-control standards for V-Series condensing units …*

**RESPONSE TO REQUEST NO. 14:**

**Relevance.** The quality allegations concern compressor-seal failure rates. Complete engineering drawings, the full Bill of Materials, and manufacturing processes for every component of every V-Series model (V-100 through V-500) have no bearing on those allegations or on Lakeshore’s exclusivity and fulfillment claims. Redfield’s manufacturing costs are likewise not relevant to any claim or defense. Lakeshore’s damages theories rest on its own lost margins and customers, not on Redfield’s cost of goods. Redfield will produce (a) published V-Series product specifications and catalog data; (b) the Quality Standards and test protocols for V-Series units; (c) all technical specifications, test protocols, and engineering data shared with Elkhorn or any other testing or certification entity; and (d) design specifications, drawings, Bill of Materials entries, supplier identity, and engineering change orders for the compressor-seal assembly and related components. Documents are being withheld in part on the basis of this objection: full-product drawings and Bill of Materials unrelated to the compressor-seal assembly, and manufacturing cost data, are being withheld.
