# Important qualifications

- Privilege determinations are document- and portion-specific. Actual distribution lists, attachments, underlying communications, corporate roles, and any joint-defense agreement should be confirmed against native files before the log is finalized.

- For mixed items, the log claims only the specifically described legal-advice/work-product passages. Nonprivileged facts and operative instructions should be produced if responsive, with any redaction narrowly tailored.

- No privilege claim is asserted here over the two Alderwood documents (RC-16 and RC-25): the engagement is a standard business audit led by Operations, expressly disclaims an attorney-client relationship, and calls the audit a non-legal professional opinion. Nor is privilege asserted over the Vasquez report to management, HR performance records, patents, the separation draft, or the nonlawyer inventor-list email based only on the present record.
