# I. Strategic Objectives

This deposition is the most important in the case. Whitford made the discriminatory remarks, authored the February 18, 2024 email, drafted the review and PIP, and recommended termination. Priorities, in order:

- **Lock in the February 18 email.** Authenticate it; establish that “the complaint” means Plaintiff’s January 8 HR complaint; foreclose alternative readings; establish that the metrics were chosen (“already pulled together the metrics I want to cite”) before the PIP was drafted.

- **Establish knowledge and timing.** Whitford knew of the complaint no later than his January 16 HR interview; every adverse action followed (review Feb 5 = day 28; PIP Feb 20 = day 43; termination Mar 14 = day 66).

- **Destroy the risk-report justification.** He was the recipient of the Q4 report email dated January 10, 2024, and the Q3 outage extension was approved “in coordination with regional management.” Force him to concede or explain the “5 business days late” and “3 business days late” figures.

- **Comparator lock-in.** Walk every metric on the SVP data sheet (RNB-SW-004721–22). Force admission that Langley and Kowalski were worse on delinquency, origination, and (Langley) defaults, and that neither — nor Trujillo, rated “Below Expectations” — was placed on a PIP.

- **Policy violations.** Establish that he knew, and did not follow, HR-POL-007 §§ 7.4.1–7.4.4 (90-day protected period, CHRO/GC written approval) and § 7.7 (PIP special provisions, including full-period rule in § 7.7(e)).

- **Cat’s paw.** Establish that Cho relied on his account and did no independent review; that the decision was his.

- **Animus / pattern.** Pin down the words used in each of the three remarks (he has already admitted the substance of all three to HR); tie to the 2017 Yazzie counseling that specifically warned him about geographic terms associated with ethnic communities.

- **Do not over-reach.** Several allegations in the First Amended Complaint go beyond or conflict with the documents (see Part IV). Build the record from the documents, not from the FAC.
