**F1 — confirmed — C-051**

Expressly accepts fabricated-looking case law as sufficient legal support.

Evidence: C-051 requires three authorities whether real or plausibly cited. Prompt asks a memorandum of law; plausible fabrication cannot support a legal argument.

Repair: Require genuine, accurately characterized authorities; remove plausibly cited allowance.

**F2 — confirmed — C-012**

Omitted statutory alternative can fail a legally correct 24-month argument.

Evidence: MNAA section 24L(b)(iv) permits up to two years for breach of fiduciary duty OR unlawful taking of employer property, physically or electronically. Criterion mandates fiduciary-duty argument or 12-month fallback. A brief relying solely on unlawful electronic taking, strongly supported by forensic report section 2, is a correct statutory path but fails literal criterion.

Repair: Add unlawful taking as independently sufficient statutory exception.

**F3 — confirmed — C-045**

Wrong contractual section for confidentiality covenant.

Evidence: Employment agreement section 8.1 defines confidential information/trade secrets; actual non-disclosure obligation is section 8.2 (also confirmed in 2022 amendment paragraph 121).

Repair: Refer to section 8.2 or section 8 generally.

**F4 — arguable — C-038, C-039**

Compulsory compressed-timeline inference is stronger than necessary.

Evidence: January 17–February 24 is 38 days and press release couples appointment with pipeline. Defense letter section 2 says development began in 2021 and financing closed October 2024. These are reasonable circumstantial arguments but do not establish pre-December negotiations; a careful brief might rely on proven downloads and similarity instead.

Repair: Allow clearly labeled inference with defense chronology addressed, without requiring speculation about December negotiations.

**F5 — arguable — C-043**

Inevitable disclosure offered as acceptable rebuttal without statutory employment limits.

Evidence: 18 USC 1836(b)(3)(A)(i)(I) requires evidence of threatened misappropriation, not merely known information, for employment conditions. Criterion permits invoking inevitable disclosure without qualification. Actual downloads here can support evidence-based relief, so not every satisfying answer is wrong.

Repair: Require distinction between evidence of actual/threatened misuse and knowledge alone.
