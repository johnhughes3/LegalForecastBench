## F1 — confirmed legal defect

HydraCore LLC citizenship depends on every member and nested member, not formation state or headquarters. Packet gives Indiana LLC/headquarters but no member citizenship. Requiring affirmative complete diversity is unsupported.

Evidence: engagement-letter.docx p16(b); no member citizenship located in packet.

## F2 — confirmed legal overstatement

Criteria: C-017

UCC2-719(3) prima-facie personal-injury unconscionability applies to consumer goods. This industrial press is commercial equipment. Defective goods is not an interchangeable statutory category. Non-signatory employees have strong separate grounds against binding them, but criterion invites wrong statutory shortcut.

Evidence: purchase-order.docx industrial press description; Ohio1302.93(C).

Repair: Preserve consumer-goods limitation; assess non-signatory status and other applicable public policy separately.

## F3 — confirmed legal/date defect

Criteria: C-066

Four-year warranty accrual ordinarily starts at tender of delivery, not acceptance; supplied record states delivery November22,2024 and acceptance December16,2024. Rubric mandates December16,2028 without analyzing tender/installation or explicit future-performance warranty.

Evidence: forensic-report.docx p67; ironclad-standard-terms.docx §10; Baker v DEC explains tender rather than acceptance.

Repair: Accept qualified accrual analysis, earliest protective November22,2028 date, contractual shortening and future-performance issues; do not require acceptance date.

## F4 — arguable mandatory conclusion

Criteria: C-041

Choice-of-law clause expressly covers all disputes related to products, not only contract. Ohio law may govern non-signatory injury claims, but Dalton tort claims require clause-scope and conflicts analysis. A categorical contract/tort divide is not compelled.

Evidence: ironclad-standard-terms.docx p89 (§15.1).

Repair: Distinguish Dalton from non-signatories; accept reasoned application of broad clause.

## F5 — confirmed source-law inconsistency

Criteria: C-026, C-027, C-073

Source purports to issue private-employer OSHA citations and require contest/payment to Ohio Division of Safety & Hygiene. Ohio has no OSHA-approved State Plan; federal OSHA covers this private workplace. Rubric rewards source recitation without acknowledging defective issuing authority.

Evidence: osha-citations.docx p120,p123,p126; incident/engagement identify private manufacturer.

Repair: Correct synthetic citation authority and preserve federal contest procedure; accept source qualification.
