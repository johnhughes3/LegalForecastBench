### F1 — Luna [C-001](https://github.com/harveyai/harvey-labs/blob/1dd81403b2fbb60596f7aea3fcecafad7bf73143/tasks/litigation-dispute-resolution/analyze-counterparty-motion-to-dismiss/task.json#L20): faithful literal grading of a defective legal requirement (high confidence)

Both judges correctly observe Luna does not call Twombly/Iqbal and Rule 9(b) inconsistent. GPT says “Twombly/Iqbal is not called out in this context.” Sonnet says the memo supplies particularity but not the demanded inconsistency. Actual Luna P98 says knowledge/intent “may be alleged generally, subject to plausibility,” identifies speakers, communications, exact assertions and harm, and says the motion's claim that no specific statements exist is contradicted by the pleading. P101–102 separately analyze plausible scienter.

The rubric demands an “inconsistency” between plausibility and particularity. Those requirements coexist. Iqbal explains that Rule 9's allowance for general mental-state allegations does not displace Rule 8's substantive plausibility requirement ([Supreme Court opinion, part IV.C](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/07-1015.ZO.html)). Motion source P215–219 invokes plausibility/scienter and a Rule 9(b) footnote. Its failure to engage particulars is a fair target; merely applying both standards is not. The failed row therefore does not demonstrate deficient legal analysis. Repair criterion to credit accurate particularity/plausibility analysis and criticism of conclusory treatment.

### M2 — Opus P308 contradicts the text of UCC §2.725 and its own P220 (moderate, definite overstatement)

P308 says §2.725 “does not support a one-year period.” The statute expressly permits parties to reduce the default four years to not less than one year ([official Business and Commerce Code PDF, §2.725(a)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/SDocs/BUSINESSANDCOMMERCECODE.pdf)). Opus P220 correctly recognizes the one-year floor, so this is an internal inconsistency. Its separate argument that this SaaS contract is a service transaction may make Article2 inapplicable, but does not justify saying the provision cannot support one year. Contract characterization/application was not exhaustively researched. This is not the defect for which [C-016](https://github.com/harveyai/harvey-labs/blob/1dd81403b2fbb60596f7aea3fcecafad7bf73143/tasks/litigation-dispute-resolution/analyze-counterparty-motion-to-dismiss/task.json#L140) failed.

### M3 — Opus blanket without-prejudice recommendation is overbroad (moderate, definite legal overstatement)

P337 says “Dismissal, if any, must be without prejudice”; P72 says a first12(b)(6) dismissal is ordinarily without prejudice. Jurisdiction/venue dismissals differ from merits dismissals. Opus P71 correctly cites Wagner and recommends seeking leave, which mitigates the practical risk but contradicts the blanket instruction. A counseled plaintiff need not receive sua sponte leave before with-prejudice dismissal; see [Eleventh Circuit application of Wagner, June12,2023, p9](https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/unpub/files/202213128.pdf). This is outside the rubric's tested propositions.
