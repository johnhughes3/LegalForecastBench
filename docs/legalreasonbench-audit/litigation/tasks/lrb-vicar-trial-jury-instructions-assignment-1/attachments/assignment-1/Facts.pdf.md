STATEMENT OF THE CASE  
 A.  Facts  
1. OTB forms in 2019.  
  
In Spring and Summer 2020, Des Moines saw an uptick in 
gangrelated crime. (TT-II 331-39).1 There were two main neighborhood 
gangs operating in Des Moines at that time: Heavy Hitters and C-Block. 
(Id.) Heavy Hitters and C-Block were rivals and opposition gangs, or 
“opps.” (Id. at 486.) Each of those gangs had several subsets, as is common 
for neighborhood-based gangs. (Id. at 268-69.)   
Under Heavy Hitters were subsets like 500, OTB, and I Kill 
Everybody (IKE). (TT-II 331, 486.) And under C-Block were subsets like 
Pop Out Gang (POG), East Side Crips, and No Good Gang. (Id.) These

500 (or what was left of them) and Heavy Hitters. The name changed, but

 B.  Procedural history  
On May 19, 2024, a grand jury returned a fifteen-count indictment 
charging several defendants, including Patton, Mallory, and Green, with 
crimes arising out of the May and December 2020 shootings. Specifically, 
Patton and Mallory were charged with one count of attempted murder in 
aid of racketeering (Count 4) and one count of using, carrying, and 
discharging a firearm during and in relation to that crime of violence 
(Count 5). (R. Doc. 2.) Patton was also charged with one count of felon in 
possession of a firearm for his possession of the firearm on May 10, 2020 
(Count 6). (R. Doc. 2.) On February 3, 2025, Patton pled guilty to Count 
6. (R. Doc. 210.)   
Green was charged with two counts of attempted murder in aid of 
racketeering (Counts 8 and 12); two counts of using, carrying, and 
discharging a firearm during and in relation to those crimes of violence 
(Counts 9 and 13); two counts of felon in possession  of ammunition

Patton, Mallory, and Green proceeded to trial on April 4, 2025. (R. 
Doc. 160.) At trial, the government introduced hundreds of exhibits, 
including evidence from the defendants’ social media accounts, photos 
from the charged and uncharged shootings, surveillance footage 
capturing some of the events, and the defendants’ prior statements. (R. 
Doc. 374.) The government also presented testimony from forty -two 
witnesses, including law enforcement, civilians, victims, and OTB 
members and associates involved in the shootings. (R. Doc. 383.) The jury 
convicted all three defendants on all counts. (R. Doc. 375, 377, 379.) The 
defendants unsuccessfully made oral and written motions for new trials 
and judgments of acquittal. (R. Doc. 399, 400, 401, 475.) Patton was 
sentenced to a total of 300 months (R. Doc. 480); Mallory a total of 192 
months (R. Doc. 483); and Green a total of 408 months (R. Doc. 486).
