METROPOLITAN POLICE DEPARTMENT
Human Resource Management Bureau | Disciplinary Review Division
MEMORANDUM
TO: Sandra M. Delgado, Assistant Chief, Professional Development Bureau
FROM: Yolanda Britt, Director, Disciplinary Review Division
RE: Comparator Discipline Cases, Adverse Action Case No. IS-23-002841 (Braddock)
DATE: April 11, 2024
1.  You  asked  the  Division  to  identify  closed  adverse  action  cases  involving  sworn 
members disciplined for unjustified force involving a firearm or serious bodily injury, for use in  
the penalty analysis in the above-referenced case. The Division identified the four cases below  
from records covering calendar years 2018 through 2021. Each resulted in termination.
2. Comparator A. Officer Terrence Guzman, Fourth District (Case No. IS-18-001922). In  
August 2018, while on duty, Officer Guzman fired three rounds at a fleeing, unarmed theft  
suspect in an alley, striking the suspect once in the leg. The Use of Force Review Board found  
the discharge outside policy. Officer Guzman had two prior sustained complaints for excessive  
force (2015 and 2017). He was terminated effective January 4, 2019.
3. Comparator B. Officer Monique Sadler, Seventh District (Case No. IS-19-002408). In  
November 2019, while on duty and responding to a noise complaint, Officer Sadler struck a  
handcuffed arrestee in the head with her service weapon, causing a skull fracture and permanent  
hearing loss. She had no prior discipline. She was terminated effective June 12, 2020.
4. Comparator C. Officer Alan Pietrzak, Second District (Case No. IS-20-001174). In  
May 2020, while off duty and intoxicated at a family gathering in the District, Officer Pietrzak  
brandished his service pistol during an argument and fired one round that struck and killed a 31-
year-old guest. The Commonwealth declined prosecution after a finding of accidental discharge  
could not be excluded. He was terminated effective February 26, 2021.
5. Comparator D. Officer Craig Voss, First District (Case No. IS-21-000866). In March  
2021, while on duty, Officer Voss deployed a chokehold prohibited by Department policy on a  
non-resisting subject during a stop, rendering the subject unconscious and causing a brain injury  
requiring hospitalization. Officer Voss had one prior sustained complaint for failure to render aid  
(2019). He was terminated effective October 8, 2021.
6. Case files for each comparator are available for your review on request. The Division  
expresses no view on the appropriate penalty in the Braddock matter.
