## R2: Criterion 12 requires the plaintiff's affidavit to swear that DuPage is inconvenient, with no record basis and no Langenhorst requirement

Status: **arguable**; category: `unsupported_fact`; criteria: criterion_12.

This consolidates the criterion_12 parts of Sol G2 and Opus O2. Criterion 12 (+5) requires identifying that 'the affidavit by the Plaintiff, Sara Davis, indicates that DuPage County would be an inconvenient forum for her ... as required under Langenhorst.' The record says only that Sara resides in Kane County. No document describes any burden on her of litigating in DuPage. Langenhorst also does not require a plaintiff's affidavit. It places on the defendant the burden of showing that the plaintiff's chosen forum is inconvenient to the defendant, and says the defendant 'cannot assert that the plaintiff's chosen forum is inconvenient to the plaintiff.' The physicians' affidavits differ: the supervisor relayed that each doctor finds DuPage inconvenient, so drafting those affidavits is grounded in the record. For Sara, a careful drafter may decline to put a sworn statement of inconvenience in the client's mouth, or may prepare a proposed affidavit conditioned on her confirmation. The second approach could satisfy the criterion, so the issue is arguable rather than problematic. Criterion 11 (support from her affidavit) is not defective because a residence affidavit is supported by the record.
