METROPOLITAN POLICE DEPARTMENT
Human Resource Management Bureau | Labor Relations Unit
MEMORANDUM
TO: Outside Counsel, PERB Case No. 25-A-09 (Braddock)
FROM: Marcus T. Bell, Labor Relations Liaison
RE: Supplemental Comparator Case Identified After Close of Arbitration Record
DATE: February 24, 2026
1. In preparing the litigation hold for a potential petition for review, this Unit identified a  
closed disciplinary case involving a firearm discharge that was not included in the Disciplinary  
Review Division’s April 17, 2024 comparator memorandum. The case was resolved through a  
negotiated  settlement  agreement  and  was  tracked  in  the  Labor  Relations  Unit’s  settlement  
database rather than the Division’s adverse action database, which is why the Division’s records  
search did not capture it. Counsel asked to be informed of any such material immediately. The  
details follow.
2. Sergeant Dana Kucera, Third District (Case No. LR-22-000419). At approximately  
11:15 p.m. on October 22, 2022, Sergeant Kucera, off duty at her residence in Prince George’s  
County, Maryland, heard glass break and observed a man reaching into the passenger window of  
her  personal  vehicle.  Sergeant  Kucera  retrieved  her  service  pistol  from  inside  her  home,  
advanced to the driveway, identified herself as a police officer, and fired one round at the man as  
he turned toward her. The round missed. The man, who was unarmed, fled and was arrested by  
Prince George’s County police two blocks away. Sergeant Kucera did not call 9-1-1 before the  
discharge.
3. The Use of Force Review Board found the discharge outside policy. The Department  
proposed  a  60-day  suspension.  Under  a  settlement  agreement  executed  on  June  30,  2023,  
Sergeant  Kucera  accepted  a  40-day  suspension  without  pay  and  a  one-year  supervisory  
probation,  and  waived  her  grievance  rights.  Sergeant  Kucera  had  no  prior  discipline.  The  
Department sustained the penalty and the matter was never grieved, arbitrated, or appealed.
4. This case was not part of the record before Arbitrator Larrabee or the Public Employee  
Relations Board. Please advise how you intend to treat it in connection with the contemplated  
petition.
