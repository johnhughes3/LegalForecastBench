# Resolved post-recovery direct queue authority v4

`legalforecast.resolved_post_recovery_public_document.v4` represents a recovered public document whose paid request was submitted directly to CourtListener RECAP Fetch and therefore has no broker receipt history.

The record is accepted only through the verifier-issued recovered-public capability. Its `delivery_authority` is `authenticated_direct_courtlistener_queue`, and its closed `direct_queue_delivery_authority` object has exactly these fields: `schema_version`, `source_provider`, `purchase_status`, `operation_key`, `queue_id`, `reservation_id`, `reservation_usd`, `queue_response_sha256`, `purchase_policy_sha256`, `purchase_operation_sha256`, `purchase_response_sha256`, `recovery_run_card_sha256`, `recovery_manifest_sha256`, `recovery_restriction_evidence_sha256`, and `purchase_state_sha256`.

The authority requires provider `courtlistener.recap-fetch+pacer`, purchase status `queued`, a queue ID that is a positive integer without leading zeros, a `reservation_usd` amount with exactly two fractional digits and no leading zeros, and reservation ID `direct:<operation_key>`. The purchase response must contain exactly `source_provider`, `reservation_usd`, `queue_id`, and `reservation_id`, with only the already ledger-validated `courtlistener_url_commitment_correction` permitted as an additional field. Presence of `broker_receipts`, including an empty list, is contradictory and rejected; no broker field is synthesized in the resolved record.

The resolver reconstructs the proof from the authenticated historical purchase operation and recovery artifacts, compares it with the opaque capability, and then binds the same proof into the resolved record and recovered-public clearance lineage. Provider, status, queue identity, reservation identity or amount, queue-response digest, purchase policy, operation, response, purchase state, or recovery artifact mutations fail before output publication or journal clearance.
