# Exact-100 document-repair executor v1

`legalforecast.exact100_document_repair_execution.v1` is a provider-neutral bridge from one authenticated selector-bearing v2 full repair plan, optionally narrowed by its exact five-case pilot projection, to existing acquisition primitives. It replays candidate byte digests from a docket-snapshot manifest pinned by an independently hashed source-lineage artifact, resolves exactly one RECAP document for each planned docket entry plus authenticated `document_selector` (`main_document` or `attachment_N`; main identity follows `attachment_number`, not the populated PACER `document_number`), preserves the selector and approved free-versus-paid route through operations, acquisition results, receipts, and successor evidence, and emits a `MissingCoreBudgetPlan` with an explicit `$3.00` reservation and the applicable pilot or full-plan aggregate cap. The execution records `scope=pilot` with the pilot digest or `scope=full_plan` with the full-plan digest; the full-plan form must cover every approved acquisition item.

Before paid execution, the bridge consumes an independently approved `legalforecast.case_dev_purchase_policy.v2` and emits `legalforecast.exact100_document_repair_purchase_authority.v1`, which binds the execution digest and scope to that fresh-ledger authority. The approval's ordered selected-candidate and purchase-document commitments, counts, projected cost, cohort-policy lineage, `$3.00` per-document reservation, and remaining global and per-case headroom must reproduce the exact repair execution. Legacy v1 policies are rejected. After the established journal initializer creates the canonical ledger, the bridge read-only verifies that journal's immutable initialization receipt and policy lineage and returns an in-memory runtime capability; the runner requires that capability before any paid callback is reachable. The bridge does not mint approval, acknowledge fees, or construct a provider.

The dependency-injected runner invokes the resolved operations in their authenticated free-first order and records a monotonic duration around each call. Its callback boundary accepts results only for the exact RECAP identity, emits byte/hash/length evidence for successful material, and stops without calling any later operation after an unknown paid result.

`legalforecast.exact100_document_repair_receipt.v1` records the exact ordered execution outcomes. The receipt envelope binds the full-plan, pilot, and execution identities. Every row binds the docket snapshot, candidate, docket entry, document selector, and RECAP document identities and records route, disposition, retry count, monotonic duration, committed cost, and retry permission. An unknown paid outcome must retain its full approved reservation, is permanently nonretryable, and deterministically marks every later planned operation `not_attempted_after_unknown`.

These sidecars do not independently seal the repaired corpus. Only a full-plan execution may seal the exact-100 successor. The sealing adapter requires a complete execution receipt in which every row is `included` or `excluded`, rejecting unknown, unattempted, and provider-error states; requires each receipt disposition to match its supplied document or exclusion evidence; rechecks every acquired document against the resolved RECAP identity; and delegates exact hash/length and semantic role validation to the missing-document successor before emitting the separate immutable exact-100 successor artifact.
