# Exact-100 document-repair executor v1

`legalforecast.exact100_document_repair_execution.v1` is a provider-neutral bridge from one authenticated full repair plan, optionally narrowed by its exact five-case pilot projection, to existing acquisition primitives. It replays candidate byte digests from a docket-snapshot manifest pinned by an independently hashed source-lineage artifact, resolves exactly one RECAP document for each planned docket entry plus authenticated `document_selector` (`main_document` or `attachment_N`; main identity follows `attachment_number`, not the populated PACER `document_number`), preserves the selector and approved free-versus-paid route through operations, acquisition results, receipts, and successor evidence, and emits a `MissingCoreBudgetPlan` with an explicit `$3.00` reservation and the applicable pilot or full-plan aggregate cap. The execution records `scope=pilot` with the pilot digest or `scope=full_plan` with the full-plan digest; the full-plan form must cover every approved acquisition item.

Before paid execution, the bridge emits `legalforecast.exact100_document_repair_purchase_authority.v1`, which binds the execution digest and scope to a fresh-ledger legacy purchase policy derived from the exact repair budget. The policy's per-document reservation must equal `$3.00`, and its remaining global and per-case headroom must cover the exact emitted budget. The bridge does not acknowledge fees or construct a provider; callers pass the verified policy and budget to the established ledgered RECAP Fetch executor.

The dependency-injected runner invokes the resolved operations in their authenticated free-first order and records a monotonic duration around each call. Its callback boundary accepts results only for the exact RECAP identity, emits byte/hash/length evidence for successful material, and stops without calling any later operation after an unknown paid result.

`legalforecast.exact100_document_repair_receipt.v1` records the exact ordered execution outcomes. Every row binds the full-plan, pilot, execution, docket snapshot, candidate, docket entry, and RECAP document identities and records route, disposition, retry count, monotonic duration, committed cost, and retry permission. An unknown paid outcome must retain its full approved reservation, is permanently nonretryable, and deterministically marks every later planned operation `not_attempted_after_unknown`.

These sidecars do not independently seal the repaired corpus. Only a full-plan execution may seal the exact-100 successor. The sealing adapter requires a complete execution receipt with no unknown or unattempted state, requires each receipt disposition to match its supplied document or exclusion evidence, rechecks every acquired document against the resolved RECAP identity, and delegates exact hash/length and semantic role validation to the missing-document successor before emitting the separate immutable exact-100 successor artifact.
