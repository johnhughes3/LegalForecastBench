# Exact-100 document-repair executor v1

`legalforecast.exact100_document_repair_execution.v1` is a provider-neutral bridge from one authenticated full repair plan and its exact five-case pilot projection to existing acquisition primitives. It binds every pilot candidate's exact docket-snapshot bytes, requires exactly one main RECAP document for each planned docket entry, preserves the approved free-versus-paid route, and emits a `MissingCoreBudgetPlan` with an explicit `$3.00` reservation and the pilot's approved aggregate cap.

Before paid execution, the bridge verifies an existing typed purchase policy. The policy's per-document reservation must equal `$3.00`, and its remaining global and per-case headroom must cover the exact emitted budget. The bridge does not mint purchase approval, initialize a ledger, acknowledge fees, or construct a provider; callers pass the verified policy and budget to the established ledgered RECAP Fetch executor.

The dependency-injected runner invokes the resolved operations in their authenticated free-first order and records a monotonic duration around each call. Its callback boundary accepts results only for the exact RECAP identity, emits byte/hash/length evidence for successful material, and stops without calling any later operation after an unknown paid result.

`legalforecast.exact100_document_repair_receipt.v1` records the exact ordered execution outcomes. Every row binds the full-plan, pilot, execution, docket snapshot, candidate, docket entry, and RECAP document identities and records route, disposition, retry count, monotonic duration, committed cost, and retry permission. An unknown paid outcome retains its approved reservation, is permanently nonretryable, and deterministically marks every later planned operation `not_attempted_after_unknown`.

These sidecars do not independently seal the repaired corpus. The sealing adapter requires a complete execution receipt with no unknown or unattempted state, rechecks every acquired document against the resolved RECAP identity, and delegates exact hash/length and semantic role validation to the missing-document successor before emitting the separate immutable exact-100 successor artifact.
